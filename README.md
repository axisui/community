# AxisUI Community

Welcome to the **AxisUI Community**!

This repository is the central place for discussions, questions, ideas, and feedback related to AxisUI.

We believe great ideas grow through collaboration. Whether you're interested in our work, have a question, want to share feedback, or simply want to connect with our team, you're welcome here.

## What Can You Do Here?

- **Ask Questions** — Have questions about AxisUI, our services, or our projects? Start a discussion.
- **Share Ideas** — Share suggestions, concepts, and ideas that could inspire future projects.
- **Give Feedback** — Help us improve by sharing your thoughts and experiences.
- **Connect & Collaborate** — Engage with our team and other community members.

## Community Guidelines

To keep this community welcoming and productive, please:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Keep discussions relevant and constructive.
- Share meaningful feedback and ideas.
- Avoid spam and unrelated content.
- Respect other members' privacy and intellectual property.

## Discussions

Choose the appropriate discussion category when starting a new conversation:

- **Announcements** — Official updates and news from AxisUI.
- **General** — Open conversations and community discussions.
- **Q&A** — Questions and answers.
- **Ideas & Feedback** — Suggestions, feature ideas, and feedback.
- **Show and Tell** — Share your projects and creative work.
